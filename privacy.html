HyperFlow SDK Demo · effective 28 October 2026

HyperFlow SDK Demo is published by Scott Bollt (HyperFlow). This policy explains what the app does with data.

Camera

The app processes the camera image on the iPhone in real time. It does not store images or measurements, and it never sends them anywhere.

License check

The app runs only with a HyperFlow license key. When you tap the key, when the app starts, and about every 15 minutes while you use it, the app contacts HyperFlow's license server at rapidpiv.caltech.edu and sends:

  • the license ID stored on the key and a cryptographic signature from the key;
  • a device identifier: a one-way hash of the identifier iOS gives this developer's apps on the device, which does not identify you and cannot follow you across other developers' apps;
  • the app version and platform.

The server records the time, the device identifier, the license, the result and the IP address the request came from. HyperFlow uses these records only to operate licenses: to limit how many devices use a license at once, to stop suspended or revoked licenses, and to fix problems. Device records are deleted after 30 days without a check-in, and the request log is overwritten as it fills.

What the app does not do

No advertising, analytics or tracking, no accounts, and no sale or sharing of data with anyone.

Children

The app is a tool for licensed organizations and is not directed at children.
