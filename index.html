HyperFlow SDK Demo

Live particle image velocimetry on iPhone, for organizations that license the HyperFlow SDK.

HyperFlow SDK Demo shows the HyperFlow SDK at work: it measures motion in the camera image in real time on the iPhone's GPU and draws the velocity field over the live view.

What you need

  • An iPhone with NFC running iOS 18 or later.
  • Your organization's HyperFlow license key.
  • An internet connection when you tap the key, and at least once a day while you use the app.

Unlocking the app

Open the app. When it asks for the license key, hold the key flat against the top of the back of the iPhone, near the cameras, and keep it still until the sheet confirms that the key was read. One tap licenses the iPhone for a day; after that, the app asks for a tap again.

If something goes wrong

"The license key was not tapped" or "moved away before it was read"
Tap again and hold the key still for about two seconds.
"Could not check in with the HyperFlow license server"
Connect to the internet and tap again.
"Already in use on the most iPhones and iPads it allows"
Each license allows a set number of devices at a time. The message says when the next place becomes free.
"Suspended" or "revoked"
Contact your organization or HyperFlow.
